# Disclaimer

## Disclaimer

This is a showcase repo with two working goals:

1. Explore technologies and architecture patterns such as Svelte, Rust, Firebase, and agentic RAG.
2. Push AI-assisted coding and product development to it's limits. 

Budget is tight. 

As a result, the codebase moves fast. Refactors, redesigns, and rough edges are part of the work. 
Nevertheless, clear structure, strong security, clean code, and better operating habits should evolve gradually over time.

Issues, criticism, and direct contributions are welcome.
