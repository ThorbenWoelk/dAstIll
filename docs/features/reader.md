# Reader

The reader shows the videos from the channels you follow as a morning paper. Each new video
arrives as a short article, written from its AI summary. You read one story, mark it as read, and
the next one takes its place.

## Reading

- The page leads with the newest unread story. Stories are ordered by the video's release date.
- The channel name sits above the headline. The summary's overview is the italic standfirst under
  it.
- The summary's "At a glance" points sit in a box above the article. The key points and takeaways
  follow as the body.
- "Watch the video" opens the original on YouTube.
- "Also in this edition" lists the next stories. Pick one to read it next.

## Moving between stories

The stories in a section are like pages: the newest first, older ones after it. The line above the
story shows where you are, for example "2 of 14".

- On a phone or tablet, **swipe left** for the next story and **swipe right** for the previous one.
  The story follows your finger. At the first or last story it springs back.
- Or press **Next ›** and **‹ Previous** above the story.
- On a keyboard, use `→` or `J` for the next story and `←` or `K` for the previous one.

Moving between stories does not mark anything as read. They stay in the paper until you finish
them.

## Finishing a story

Press **Mark as read**, or the `R` key. The story leaves the paper and the next one takes its place,
so you keep your place in the section.

**Undo**, or the `U` key, brings back the story you just finished.

On a phone the Mark as read bar stays at the bottom of the screen while you read.

## Highlights

Select any passage in a story and press **Highlight**. The passage is marked in yellow and stays
marked when you come back to the story. Tap or click a highlight to remove it.

**Highlights** lists every passage you marked, by story, newest stories first. Type
in the search box to find a passage by its words, the story title, or the channel name. Select a
story title to open the full story.

## Finished stories

**Finished** lists the stories you marked as read, the most recently finished
first, grouped by the day you finished them. Open one to read it again. **Mark as unread** puts it
back in the paper.

A story opened from Finished or Highlights has a back link above it, for example **‹ Finished**.
On a phone you can also swipe right to go back. This matters when the paper is installed on a
phone, where there is no browser back button.

## Sections

The front page shows every channel together. Each channel is also a section with its own unread
count. Pick a section in the bar under the masthead to read only that channel. The reader
remembers your choice in this browser.

Use **Sections** to follow a new channel or stop following one. Sign out is at
the bottom of that page. You can add a
YouTube `@handle`, a channel link, or a podcast feed URL. Removing a section stops following the
channel. It does not delete anything for other readers.

## Getting around on a phone

The pages of the paper (Front page, Highlights, Finished, Sections) are in a bar along the bottom
of the screen, in easy reach of your thumb. On a wider screen they are links at the top right.

While you scroll down a story, the bottom bar and the section bar slide away so the story has the
whole screen. Scroll up a little and both come back: the section bar at the top of the screen, the
page bar at the bottom. Mark as read always stays.

## Fresh stories

The paper loads when you open it. It reloads when you press the refresh icon, or when you come back
to the tab after 30 minutes. It does not poll in the background.

The last edition is kept in your browser, so the paper opens at once and fills in new stories when
they arrive. Signing out removes it.

## Light and dark

The reader follows your system setting: newsprint by day, a dark night edition otherwise.

## Install on a phone

Open the reader in your phone's browser and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens like an
app.
